# Product

## Register

Operate. LeadFlow is a working tool: a sales rep opens it many times a day to see who to call, log what was said, and set the next follow-up.

## Users

Individual sales reps and small-team account executives. They work in daylight office conditions, often between calls, and need to scan a list in seconds and log a note in under ten.

## Purpose

A lightweight CRM on a single screen: a lead list (status, last note, time since last discussion, today's follow-ups pinned, overdue ones in red), a timeline dialog per lead (full discussion history, add note with optional follow-up, change status) and a quick way to add a lead.

## Brand and tone

Calm, precise, trustworthy, like a well-kept paper ledger. Warm neutrals, one deep green accent, red reserved for overdue work. Plain verbs in copy ("Log note", "Add lead").

## Anti-references

Generic blue SaaS dashboards, dark neon CRMs, card grids of equal tiles, gradient text, decorative glass.

## Assumptions

Written from the brief and wireframes without a live interview (the brief fixed scope and flows); revise if the product direction changes.
